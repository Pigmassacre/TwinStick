FIREDAMP
A game by Pigmassacre


It began in 2015 as a libGDX game, with a ranger, a lamp and a stick of dynamite, and was never finished. The ranger, the planks and the dirt are the original ones.

Made with Godot Engine (godotengine.org). Its notices are in GODOT_COPYRIGHT.txt.

Sounds: Ovani Sound's sound packs (Gothic Fantasy, Explosives, Fire, Metal, Earth, Foley, Cozy Casual, Hollywood Action, Body Horror, Air, Animals, Retro Gaming) and FilmCow's Royalty Free Sound Effects Library.
