Firedamp

A game by Pigmassacre.

It began in 2015 as a libGDX game, with a ranger, a lamp and a stick of
dynamite, and was never finished. The ranger, the planks and the dirt are the
original ones.

Made with Godot Engine (godotengine.org); its notices are in GODOT_COPYRIGHT.txt.

Lettering: Bungee, copyright 2023 The Bungee Project Authors (David Jonathan
Ross), under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Its licence is in Bungee-OFL.txt.

Music: Ovani Sound. Fantasy Music Pack Vol. 2 ("Mysterious Exploration", "Evil
Cave", "Small Boss", "Death"), Epic Music Pack ("Underworld", "Bully"),
Orchestral Ambient Music Pack Vol. 1 ("Rest Now").

Sounds: Ovani Sound's sound packs (Gothic Fantasy, Explosives, Fire, Metal,
Earth, Foley Inventory, Foley Props, Foley Sports, Foley Movement, Cozy Casual,
Hollywood Action, Body Horror, Air, Animals, Steampunk, Retro Gaming), and
FilmCow's Royalty Free Sound Effects Library.
